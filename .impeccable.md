## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Job seekers, software engineers, and digital professionals tracking job opportunities.
- **Context of Use**: Focused sessions checking pipeline, updating interview stages, logs, and recruiters, demanding clarity under job search stress.
- **Emotional Goals**: Control, confidence, clarity, organization, reduced search fatigue.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Clean, systematic, structured, highly utility-driven.
- **Personality**: Utilitarian minimalism, solid typographic hierarchy, robust layout structure.
- **Explicit Exclusions**: No AI neon-on-dark overlays, no meaningless floating elements, no glassmorphism for pure decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Industrial minimalist, dark-themed neutral workspace with deliberate semantic stage accents.
- **Theme**: Dark mode default (neutral-950 base) with clean, high-contrast typography and subtle system spacing.
- **Aesthetic Refinement**: Flat solid sheets, crisp 1px borders, high readability type scales, strong spatial rhythm.

### Design Principles
1. **Utility First**: UI elements must earn their presence through clear function.
2. **Visual Restraint**: Reduce decorative indicators (like radial neon glows, glassmorphism) in favor of semantic colors and flat panels.
3. **Impeccability**: Pixel-perfect spacing, fluid layout adaptation, accessible typography, and keyboard responsiveness.
